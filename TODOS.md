## Product

### Build a consented long-term health archive

**What:** Add an account-bound health timeline that can preserve user-approved health facts, tongue-photo changes, and related conversations across devices.

**Why:** Seven-day device memory improves short-term continuity but cannot provide a durable, user-controlled health history.

**Context:** This review deliberately keeps the first release to encrypted seven-day text memory and excludes tongue images/results. The next phase must use authenticated account ownership, explicit consent, source dates, re-confirmation of stale health facts, and user-visible view, correct, delete, retention, and access-history controls. Do not turn the temporary token into a hidden permanent profile.

**Effort:** XL
**Priority:** P2
**Depends on:** Stable seven-day-memory production results; authenticated account identity; approved privacy, retention, deletion, and cross-device rules.
